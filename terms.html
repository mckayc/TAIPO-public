What Taipo is

Taipo is a browser extension that reads pages you're already logged into — Amazon Associates, Amazon Influencer, Amazon Creator Connections, and optionally your own YouTube channel — and organizes what it finds into a dashboard. It can also automate repetitive page visits on a schedule you set up yourself.

It's provided free, with no account, subscription, or payment involved.

Your responsibility

You're responsible for how you use Taipo, including:

  • Keeping your own login credentials and account access secure.
  • Reviewing any data Taipo surfaces before relying on it for a real decision — see Section 4.
Taipo doesn't act on your Amazon account's behalf in any way Amazon itself wouldn't attribute directly to you — it reads pages and reloads them the same way you would by clicking around yourself, just automated.
