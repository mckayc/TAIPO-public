Taipo
Chrome Extension

Automate the busywork of being an Amazon creator.

Taipo is a free Chrome extension that pulls your Amazon Associates, Amazon Influencer, and Creator Connections data together into one dashboard — reading pages you're already logged into, inside your own browser, on a schedule you control.

What it does

  • Tracks earnings and clicks across your Amazon Associates and Influencer accounts in one place, instead of switching between Amazon's own separate report pages.
  • Watches Creator Connections for new campaigns and commission changes.
  • Runs on a schedule you set — Chrome alarms trigger each task automatically, with no need to keep a tab open and watch it.
  • Keeps everything local by default — your data lives in your own browser, not on a server we operate. An optional Google Drive backup exists if you want it, entirely under your own control.

Who it's for

Amazon affiliates and influencers who post product videos and want a clearer, faster read on how their content and campaigns are actually performing — without manually re-checking several different Amazon report pages every day.
